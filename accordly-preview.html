What Accordly Does

Every module your clients need.
Every document you need.

Built from the ground up for high-conflict co-parenting — not adapted from generic case management software.

🧠
AI Dashboard & Parent Fitness Scoring
Dynamic compliance scores for each parent, built from communication flags, payment history, schedule adherence, and behavioral patterns. Updated continuously. Court-exportable on demand.
💬
AI Messaging & Abuse Detection
All co-parent communications are analyzed in real time. Coercive, threatening, and manipulative language is flagged with severity ratings, timestamps, and pattern documentation for court submission.
💳
Payment Plan Health Tracker
Child support and financial obligation tracking with intelligent classification: On Track, Partial Payment, or At Risk — full audit history ready for court submission at any time.
📅
Shared Custody Calendar
Parenting plan schedule tracking with deviation logging. Every missed exchange, late pickup, or schedule modification is automatically timestamped and available as compliance evidence.
📋
Court Report Generator
One-click reports that synthesize all platform data — flagged messages, payment history, calendar deviations, fitness scores — into structured, attorney-ready documentation aligned with family court standards.
⚖️
Judicial Playbook & Child Profiles
A research-backed reference module surfacing family court provisions and precedents. Paired with child profile and counseling report tools for comprehensive case documentation.
